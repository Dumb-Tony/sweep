# After Hours — restoration expansion (v0.4)

This release moves the default view to an over-the-shoulder camera and expands the first room into a longer, visible restoration process. The room camera remains available from the header for planning and screenshots.

## Restoration route

1. Wheel all three cabinets through the doorway into storage.
2. Sweep loose debris in small sections.
3. Mop the floor in small sections.
4. Pry up each old floor tile. Removed tiles remain as loose debris.
5. Collect up to 12 flooring pieces at a time and empty the bag at the front disposal bin. New flooring stays locked until every piece has been disposed of.
6. Select and install the new floor one section at a time.
7. Strip the old wallpaper along both side walls, repair the exposed plaster, then select and roll on a new color.
8. Open close-up views of all three cabinets and rub the screen, controls, and body clean by hand. Dirt also fades from the cabinet models in the room.
9. Rewire Star Signal, bring it back through the doorway, and play it.

The save format migrates existing v2 restoration saves into v3. Finished saves stay finished; earlier saves resume at an equivalent point without clearing the player's choices or arcade score.

## Camera and controls

The shoulder camera follows close behind and to one side of the character. It retracts before walls and fixtures can block it, and the character hides if a very tight corner forces the camera too close. Right drag or Q/R turns the view; movement follows that heading. The header button toggles the established room overview.

## Validation

The deterministic route covers every restoration phase, 13 required debris-disposal trips, all wall sections, all 72 cabinet-cleaning patches, repair gating, save migration, camera collision samples, and the final cabinet return. Browser-driven playtesting separately covered shoulder framing, floor removal, collection/disposal, tile installation, wall phase transitions, and the drag-to-clean cabinet view.
